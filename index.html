04 / How I work

Thoughtful process. Consistent execution.

Process spotlight / Localization

Building for different markets

Challenge: A funnel needs to feel native, not simply translated.

My approach: Adapt phrasing, spelling, currency, names, market references, and page content while checking for leftover language and inconsistent claims.

Focus: Clear, consistent, audience-appropriate experiences.

Process spotlight / Quality assurance

Reviewing before launch

Challenge: Small inconsistencies can undermine an otherwise polished funnel.

My approach: Check mobile layouts, product details, pricing, guarantee copy, CTA consistency, and alignment between advertorial and product pages.

Focus: Fewer avoidable errors and smoother handoffs.
