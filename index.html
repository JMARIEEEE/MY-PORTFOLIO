05 / How I work

My funnel-building approach

A practical workflow I use to keep pages clear, consistent, and ready for review. This section describes my process rather than claiming unverified conversion results.

Research & alignment

Review product details, target audience, competitor angles, offer structure, and copy before development. Flag contradictions early.

Build & localize

Develop responsive pages and adapt names, language, currencies, offers, and cultural references for the intended market.

QA & refine

Check desktop/mobile layouts, links, pricing, guarantees, product naming, copy accuracy, and checkout consistency before launch.
